Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Overview

This website documents a personal configuration of rclone for accessing the owner's Google Drive account. It is not a commercial service and is not intended for use by third parties.

2. Google user data accessed

The locally operated rclone software may access Google Drive files, folders, and related metadata, depending on the permissions explicitly granted by the account owner. The access permissions are determined by the OAuth scopes selected during rclone configuration.

3. How data is used

Google Drive data is used exclusively by the account owner for personal file management, synchronization, transfers, and backups initiated through rclone.

This website does not itself access Google Drive, request Google account authorization, or process Google user data.

4. Data storage and security

The rclone software stores its configuration and authentication information in accordance with its own configuration and the settings of the computer on which it runs. OAuth tokens and configuration files should be protected against unauthorized access.

Files copied or synchronized through rclone may be stored on the computer or in Google Drive, as directed by the user. Their retention depends on the user's operations and settings.

5. Sharing and disclosure

This personal project does not sell Google user data or independently disclose it to third parties. Data transfers to Google Drive occur when required by the user's file operations.

6. Third-party services

Google Drive is provided by Google, and rclone is independently developed open-source software. Their respective terms and privacy policies apply to the services they provide.
